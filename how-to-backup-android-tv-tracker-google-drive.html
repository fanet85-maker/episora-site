How to configure Google Drive backup

Official support guide • Reading time: 2 minutes

To keep your Episora-WatchMate history safe, follow these setup steps in the app menu.

Steps to create the backup are:

1. Open your profile

Open Episora-WatchMate and tap the profile icon at the top right.

2. Tap the gear icon

Also at the top right, tap the gear icon to open the settings.

3. Open App Settings

In the settings menu, select App Settings.

4. Scroll to Google Drive backup

Scroll down until you find the Google Drive backup section.

5. Sign in with your account

Sign in with your Google account to link your Drive storage to the app.

6. Set frequency and Wi-Fi only

Under Automatic backup, choose the backup frequency and whether to use Wi-Fi only.

Why enable Google Drive backup?

This keeps your tracked history safe if you lose or replace your phone and lets you restore everything quickly on a new device.
