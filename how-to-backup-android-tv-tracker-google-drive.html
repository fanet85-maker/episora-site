Setting up safe Google Drive Backup on Episora-WatchMate

Official Support Guide • Reading time: 2 minutes

Unlike old tracking platforms like TV Time, which stored all your data on proprietary centralized servers (leaving your logs vulnerable to sudden service closures), Episora-WatchMate puts user privacy first. Your complete stats, watched movies, and custom TV catalog are kept directly on your local Android device storage.

To ensure full peace of mind even if you damage, lose, or upgrade your mobile device, the app features a native, seamless **Google Drive cloud synchronization engine**. This setup guarantees that your logs remain 100% yours, managed entirely through your private Google credentials.

Steps to set up your cloud backup in under a minute:

1. Navigate to the Synchronization Menu

Launch the Episora-WatchMate app on your Android device. On the main bottom navigation bar, tap on the Profile icon located in the lower-right corner. Scroll down the option dashboard and select the tab labeled "Cloud Sync" or "Data Backup".

2. Link Your Google Account (Gmail)

Tap on the prominent "Connect Google Drive" interface button. A secure, system-native Android window prompt will appear, allowing you to choose your primary Gmail address. Select your preferred user account and authorize the standard verification permissions. The app will allocate an isolated, highly secure private partition inside your Drive storage.

3. Toggle Automatic Live Backups

Once the connection is established, you can pick your backup routine. We strongly recommend toggling the "Background Auto-Backup" setting. With this active, every time you flag a movie as completed or tap *"Mark Watched"* on a TV series episode, the application silently updates your cloud safety copy on Google Drive instantly.

How to restore data on a brand new device?

If you switch or upgrade your phone, simply download Episora-WatchMate from the Play Store again, navigate to *Profile > Cloud Sync*, and log into the exact same Gmail account. Our background manager will automatically fetch your legacy backup manifest file and prompt you to import your archive, rebuilding your layout in seconds.
