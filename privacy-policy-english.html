Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to Episora-WatchMate. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. Below, we explain in detail how your data is handled both when you browse our website and when you use our Android application.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the data collected through this website is the Episora-WatchMate development team. For any questions or requests regarding your privacy, you can contact us via our contact page.

2. Data Collected via the Website

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors interact with the site (most visited pages, general geographic origin, duration of stay), solely for the purpose of optimizing the user experience.

  • Data collected: Cookies, generic usage data, anonymized IP addresses.
  • Method: Data is collected in aggregate and anonymous form. Google does not associate your IP address with any other data held by Google.
  • Opt-out: You can disable cookies directly from your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Data Collected via the Android App (Episora-WatchMate)

Our philosophy is simple: your data belongs only to you. The Episora-WatchMate application is designed with maximum privacy in mind:

  • No Central Server: We do not upload your viewing habits or your TV show list to any central database or proprietary server.
  • Local Storage: Your entire history, watchlist, and viewing progress are stored exclusively in your Android smartphone's local memory.
  • Personal Sincronization on Google Drive: If you decide to enable the backup feature, the application will use Google APIs to save a copy of your data directly inside your personal Google Drive space. We do not have any access to this file or your Google account.

4. User Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data at any time. Since the app does not store data on our servers, you can permanently delete all your usage data simply by uninstalling the application from your device or by deleting the backup folder from your personal Google Drive.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this privacy policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
