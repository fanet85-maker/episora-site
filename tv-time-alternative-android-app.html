Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Episora-WatchMate free?

Yes, the core tracking features and personal backup are completely free. In the future, we may introduce optional paid features or ads to support the app.

Can I really recover my history from TV Time?

Absolutely yes. You can request your export directly from TV Time’s official site at gdpr.tvtime.com/gdpr/self-service and then upload the file into Episora-WatchMate following our import guide.

What happens if I switch phones?

Thanks to synchronization with your Google Drive account, you can install Episora-WatchMate on the new device, whether it is Android or iPhone, and restore the backup to instantly recover all your TV shows and movies.

Are my data safe? Who can see what I watch?

Only you. Unlike other platforms, we do not upload your watch history to a central proprietary database. Your data stays on your phone and in your personal Google Drive space, privately. In the future, we may add comments and ratings, but that will remain entirely optional.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! Once the initial catalog is synced, you can mark episodes as watched or browse your list even without an active internet connection. The app will sync the backup to Google Drive according to your configured schedule.
