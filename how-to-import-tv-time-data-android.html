How to migrate your tracking history from TV Time to Episora-WatchMate

Official Support Guide • Reading time: 2 minutes

Are you looking for a reliable TV Time alternative or a safer way to keep track of your movies and TV shows without depending on third-party cloud servers? You don't have to start from scratch and lose years of curated data tracking.

Thanks to the native migration feature embedded inside Episora-WatchMate, you can transfer your entire history seamlessly. Your personal data will be kept locally on your Android device, safe from any future service shutdowns.

The 3 steps to complete your migration:

1. Export data from TV Time

Open the TV Time application on your mobile device, navigate to your personal account profile page, and open the account settings dashboard. Find the option related to privacy or data management and request an official account data export (GDPR data protocol). You will receive an export file (usually wrapped in a .json or .csv format) delivered straight to your email or downloaded directly to your local device memory.

2. Set up Episora-WatchMate

If you haven't already, download our application from the Google Play Store. Launch the app on your Android smartphone, tap on your Profile navigation tab in the bottom right corner, and open the Advanced Settings menu. Click on the button labeled "Import External Data Backup".

3. Upload the backup file

Your Android native file manager will prompt you to choose a document: select the backup file you previously requested from TV Time. The Episora-WatchMate data parser will process your list within seconds, instantly mapping your exact custom catalog of on-going TV shows, watched movies, and completed seasons directly onto your phone layout.

Need help or troubleshooting?

Always double-check that the file extension matches the official formats provided by the external app and is not corrupted during download. Once your import is complete, we highly recommend setting up your **encrypted Google Drive cloud backup** inside our app interface to keep an additional layer of security over your local data storage.
